# Arcturus

A "Your Life in Weeks" style chart that highlights how many weeks of your life you've spent typing with a CharaChorder.

## Links

- [Website](https://andy23512.github.io/arcturus/)

## Background

[bryanbraun.com/your-life/weeks.html](https://www.bryanbraun.com/your-life/weeks.html) visualizes a life as a grid of weeks, one square per week over a 90-year span. Arcturus extends that idea for CharaChorder users: alongside your birthday, you enter the date you started using a CharaChorder chorded-keyboard device and, optionally, the date you fully switched to it. The grid then marks which weeks were lived before, during, and after that transition.

## Features

- 90-year, 52-week-per-row grid, matching the original "Your Life in Weeks" layout
- Three lived-week states — before CharaChorder, using CharaChorder, and fully switched to CharaChorder — plus unlived future weeks
- Stats panel — weeks lived, % of a 90-year life elapsed, weeks and % of life-so-far spent with CharaChorder, and (if a full-switch date is given) the same for full-switch weeks
- Printable — a dedicated print stylesheet keeps the grid, legend, and stats, and drops the form/buttons
